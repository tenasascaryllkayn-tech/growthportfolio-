Classroom Philosophy

My educational philosophy is a commitment to Ecological Progressivism, driven by a proactive Special Education (SPED) lens and operationalized through Universal Design for Learning (UDL). I believe the learning environment must bend to the child, not the child to the environment.

Summary for My Defense

"My pedagogy is driven by Ecological Progressivism filtered through a proactive SPED lens. By synthesizing the environmental intentionality of Reggio Emilia with the inclusive architecture of UDL, I transform the classroom into a living ecosystem where the scaffolding typically reserved for isolated spaces becomes the standard, beautiful design for all children."

Reggio Emilia Inspired

I honor the "100 languages of children" by providing diverse expressive mediums. I treat the physical classroom and its biophilic design as the "Third Teacher"—an active collaborator that soothes the nervous system and sparks wonder.

Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

I reject a one-size-fits-all curriculum. Instead of retrofitting modifications, I proactively design the classroom architecture from the start so that it supports and accommodates all diverse nervous systems.

Ecological Progressivism

Rooted in Arnold Gesell’s natural developmental timelines and John Dewey’s "learning by doing," I focus on grounded, tactile reality. I advocate for the "dignity of risk," allowing children to handle real tools and navigate authentic challenges to build true resilience.

Social-Emotional Learning (SEL)

Guided by Vygotsky and Bronfenbrenner, I co-construct a safe, nested community. By teaching emotional granularity, I equip children with the specific linguistic tools they need for authentic self-regulation and connection.
