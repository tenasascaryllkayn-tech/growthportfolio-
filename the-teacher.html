About Me

I’m Caryll Kayn, a professional digital content editor for LexisNexis Legal & Professional, and I’m currently taking my Master of Education degree. Balancing a demanding editing job and heavy graduate school modules takes a lot of discipline and late-night reading. But the rewarding part is getting to use what I learn to design gentler, happier, and more inclusive classrooms for young kids.

In my studies, I focus on early childhood and special education. I really believe that classrooms shouldn't be about rigid rules or forcing kids to just comply. Instead, I'm drawn to progressive ideas like the Reggio Emilia approach and the “Dignity of Risk,” which are all about creating safe, self-regulating spaces that protect a child’s natural innocence and creative expression.

Beyond the Desk & Classroom

When I’m not buried in schoolwork or editing legal content, I keep myself grounded through a mix of eclectic hobbies:

  • Navigate & Observe: I have a deep appreciation for cultural preservation, and I love keeping up with the news, always drawn to thoughts and ideas that navigate the complexities of our changing world.
  • Create & Experiment: On the creative side, I express myself through hands-on beadwork and love experimenting with creative cooking in the kitchen.
  • Quiet Companionship: Most nights, you can find me typing away at my reflections with my scruffy Shih Tzu-mix, Jinji, keeping me company right by my side.
